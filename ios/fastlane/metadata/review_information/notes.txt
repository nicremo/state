State is a client for a self-hosted service. No review account is required.

On first launch the app shows a short three screen introduction. Tap "Skip" in the top right, or step through it, to reach the connection screen. On that screen, tap "Look around without a server" at the bottom to access deterministic sample reminders, activity history and settings without a server. The demo does not transmit data.

The connection screen also links to the in-app documentation, which explains the full setup.

For a full self-hosted test, deploy the public server from this repository and enter its bootstrap token, or scan its one-time pairing code. Notification delivery uses standard local notifications first. The shared relay receives encrypted content only.
